# Creatures of Ki: Kija Renew

The Kija from Shooki's **Creatures of Ki**, rebuilt as a Biotech xenotype for the latest RimWorld version.

**I am not the author of the Kija.** Their design, their textures and their name list are Shooki's;
the 1.0 to 1.4 continuation is Mlie's. What I do here is rebuild them without Humanoid Alien Races: the
original playable race needs that framework, this mod uses the Biotech DLC instead. Credit goes to them;
mistakes in the rebuild are mine.

Original mod: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2726461020 (Mlie's continuation, last
supporting 1.4, abandoned and not withdrawn).

Companion mod: [Creatures of Ki: Teshi Renew](https://github.com/vbardales/Rimworld-Creatures-Of-Ki-Teshi-Renew),
the teshi animal and its eggs, from the same original mod. The two do not depend on each other.

## Status

Not started: the repository holds only the documentation, see [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) and

## Plan

- The Kija as a `XenotypeDef` with its genes: a gentle, deer-like humanoid, industrious with plants and
  animals, fragile in a fight (stats read from the original `Alien_Kija`).
- Their look (head, body, chest, tail) as Biotech genes, using the original textures.
- Pawn kinds, factions and name maker of the original, rebuilt on Biotech.
- Requires the Biotech DLC. No Humanoid Alien Races, no Harmony.

Content mod: removing it mid-game will lose any Kija already in play.
